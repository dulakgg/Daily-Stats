# Daily Stars

Simple mod that adds a new stat in the **garage** (Where you can change your icons) and also in the **level search page** showing the number of stars and moons you've collected **in a day**.

# Settings

There are three settings :

- **Toggle** the visibility of the stat in the **garage**
- **Toggle** the visibility of the stat in the **level search page**
- **Toggle** the visibility of the **particules** behind the daily star/moon icon
